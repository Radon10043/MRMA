Existing metamorphic relations of Triangle Area and Type Identification Algorithm are shown as follows:

a, b, c is the first triangle, and a’, b’ c’ is the second triangle.
1. (a, b, c) = (b’, a’, c’), the output will not change.
2. (a, b, c) = (a’, c’, b’), the output will not change.
3. (a, b, c) = (c’, b’, a’), the output will not change.
4. (a, b, c) = (2a’, 2b’, 2c’), then the type will not change, but the area of the first triangle is four times the second triangle.
