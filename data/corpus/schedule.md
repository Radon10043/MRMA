Existing metamorphic relations of Priority scheduler are shown as follows:

1. If the size of the a job-list is 1, then the operation that moving the job at the top of this list to the end will not affect the output.
2. Two different sets of operations: The first is adding one job at the end of the P3, while the second is adding one job at the end of P2 and then moving it to P3. They will output the same.
